Quick Safety Tips

  • 🔒 Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV or password.
  • ✅ Always verify using the official website or app.
  • 🛡️ Enable two-step verification (2FA) where available.
  • 🔑 Change compromised passwords through the official website or app.
  • 📸 Screenshot suspicious messages before deleting.
  • 🚫 Do not forward suspicious messages to others.
