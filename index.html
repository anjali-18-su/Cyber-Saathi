Already Lost Money?

If you have already lost money or shared sensitive information with a scammer, please report it immediately. Every minute counts.

ℹ️

For immediate financial cyber fraud: call 1930. For broader cybercrime reporting: visit cybercrime.gov.in. Also inform your bank or payment provider right away.

Do not call any number sent to you in a suspicious message.
