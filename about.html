About Cyber Saathi

A student-developed cyber-awareness project designed to make online safety easier to understand for everyone.

What Cyber Saathi Helps With

Cyber Saathi provides simple, practical, step-by-step guidance for people who may not have technical knowledge. It helps users recognise and respond to suspicious messages, phone calls, video calls, emails, malicious links, UPI and payment scams, fake courier messages, social media impersonation and AI-generated deepfake scams.

Our Mission

To make cyber safety easier to understand and accessible to people with different levels of digital knowledge. Cyber Saathi aims to help users recognise common online scams, take safer actions and find appropriate reporting resources when they need help.

Key Features

  • 📋 Practical scenario-based cyber safety guidance for 9 common scam types.
  • 🌐 Full support for English, Hindi and Marathi.
  • 🧠 Interactive 10-question Cyber Safety Quiz with instant feedback.
  • 🆘 Trusted cybercrime reporting resources including 1930 helpline and cybercrime.gov.in.
  • 👥 Simple instructions designed for non-technical users.
  • 📱 Responsive design — works on desktop and mobile devices.

Technology

Cyber Saathi is built entirely with plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or external dependencies. Content is localised using JSON-based language files, and language preference is saved using browser localStorage. It can be deployed for free on any static hosting service.
